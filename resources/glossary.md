# Glossary

Key terms and concepts for understanding Perplexity and effective prompting.

---

## A

**Academic Mode**
Focus mode in Perplexity that prioritizes peer-reviewed sources and scholarly databases.

---

## F

**Few-Shot Prompting**
Providing examples in your prompt to guide the model. Effective in ChatGPT but counterproductive in Perplexity (over-constrains search).

**Focus Mode**
Perplexity feature that changes search behavior to target specific types of sources (Academic, Social, Finance, Video, etc.).

---

## H

**Hallucination**
When an AI generates false or unsupported information that sounds plausible. Both Perplexity and ChatGPT can hallucinate, but for different reasons.

---

## L

**Labs**
Perplexity feature for creating automated, formatted reports with standing instructions.

---

## P

**Parametric Knowledge**
Information stored in an AI model's weights from training. ChatGPT relies on this; Perplexity does not.

**Progressive Deepening**
Strategy of starting with broad queries and iteratively refining based on results.

---

## R

**RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation)**
Architecture that combines internet search (retrieval) with AI synthesis (generation). Perplexity's core approach.

**Research Mode**
Perplexity feature that performs multiple search passes and reads hundreds of sources for comprehensive analysis.

---

## S

**Spaces**
Perplexity workspaces with standing instructions for recurring research workflows.

**Source Triangulation**
Verifying claims by finding multiple independent sources that agree.

---

## T

**Temporal Comparison**
Comparing how information, understanding, or claims have changed over time.
