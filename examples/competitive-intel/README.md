# Competitive Intelligence Examples

Real-world prompts for business analysis and competitive research.

---

## Example 1: Company Strategy Analysis

### Scenario
Understanding a competitor's strategic direction.

### Prompt
```
Analyze [Company]'s strategic direction based on:
- Last 4 quarterly earnings calls
- Product launches in past 12 months
- Executive statements and interviews
- Analyst reports from major firms

Identify:
- Core strategic priorities
- Market positioning changes
- Investment areas
- Competitive threats they're addressing

Cite specific sources with dates.
```

## Example 2: Product Comparison

### Scenario
Comparing competing products feature-by-feature.

### Prompt
```
Create a comparison table of [Product Category] from:
[Company A], [Company B], [Company C]

Columns:
- Pricing (all tiers)
- Key features
- Target customer
- Unique differentiators
- Recent updates (last 6 months)
- Customer sentiment (from reviews)

Use official product pages and recent reviews.
Cite all claims.
```

## Example 3: Market Entry Analysis

### Scenario
Researching a new market you're considering entering.

### Prompt Sequence

**Round 1: Market Size**
```
Market size and growth rate for [product/service] in [region]
after 2023.
Include TAM, SAM, SOM if available.
Cite market research firms and industry reports.
```

**Round 2: Competitive Landscape**
```
Who are the top 5 players in [market]?
For each:
- Market share
- Positioning (premium/budget/etc.)
- Key strengths
- Recent funding or M&A activity
```

**Round 3: Barriers to Entry**
```
What are the main barriers to entering [market]?
- Regulatory requirements
- Capital requirements
- Network effects or switching costs
- Incumbent advantages

Cite specific examples and sources.
```

**Round 4: Success Patterns**
```
What companies successfully entered [market] in last 5 years?
What strategies did they use?
Which failed and why?
```

## Example 4: Pricing Strategy Research

### Scenario
Understanding how competitors price their products.

### Prompt
```
Analyze pricing strategies for [product category]:

For [Company A], [Company B], [Company C]:
- Pricing tiers and what's included
- Pricing model (subscription, usage-based, etc.)
- Recent price changes
- Discounting or promotional strategies
- Enterprise vs SMB pricing differences

Use official pricing pages and recent announcements.
Note date of information.
```

## Example 5: Funding and Investment Tracking

### Scenario
Tracking competitor funding and investor sentiment.

### Prompt
```
[Company] funding history and investor activity:

Find:
- All funding rounds (amounts, dates, investors)
- Valuation at each round
- Use of funds (from announcements)
- Investor statements about the company
- Recent insider buying/selling (if public)

Focus on last 3 years.
Cite press releases and SEC filings.
```

## Tips for Competitive Intelligence

### ✅ DO:
- Use multiple source types (official + third-party)
- Note dates (competitive landscape changes fast)
- Distinguish facts from speculation
- Check for bias in sources
- Track changes over time
- Verify with official sources

### ❌ DON'T:
- Rely on single sources
- Trust outdated information
- Confuse marketing with reality
- Ignore customer sentiment
- Skip verifying claims
- Forget to cite sources
